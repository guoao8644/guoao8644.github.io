<h2 id="hosei">Hosei University, Graduate School of Science and Engineering (2024 – Present)</h2>

**[Generative AI with Large Language Models](https://syllabus.hosei.ac.jp/web/preview.php?no_id=2514978&nendo=2025&gakubueng=EV&t_mode=pc&radd=)** (大規模言語モデルを用いた生成型AI)

A graduate course newly established in 2024. It covers the core principles of large language models (LLMs), hands-on practice in implementing, fine-tuning and evaluating them, and their applications in research areas such as dialogue systems, and concludes with a final project in which students build and present their own LLM-based applications. The course aims to give students a comprehensive understanding of generative AI centered on LLMs and to enable them to apply LLMs in their own research.
