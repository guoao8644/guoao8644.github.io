<h2 id="hosei">Hosei University, Graduate School of Science and Engineering (2024 – Present)</h2>

**[Generative AI with Large Language Models](https://syllabus.hosei.ac.jp/web/preview.php?no_id=2514978&nendo=2025&gakubueng=EV&t_mode=pc&radd=)** (大規模言語モデルを用いた生成型AI) &nbsp;·&nbsp; Part-time Lecturer, sole instructor &nbsp;·&nbsp; graduate course, fall semester, 2 credits

A graduate course that I designed and teach independently. It gives students a comprehensive understanding of generative AI centered on large language models (LLMs), combining the core principles of LLMs with hands-on practice in implementing, fine-tuning and evaluating them, and shows how they are applied in research areas such as dialogue systems. Students build their own LLM-based applications and present a final project, so that they can apply LLMs to their own research.
