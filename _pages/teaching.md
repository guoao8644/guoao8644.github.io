<h2 id="nagoya">Nagoya University, School of Informatics (2026 – Present)</h2>

- **[Computer Science Experiment a](https://syllabus.adm.nagoya-u.ac.jp/data/2026/10_2026_X320001001369.html)** (コンピュータ科学実験a): laboratory course in which students design logic circuits and a MIPS processor in a hardware description language, run them on an FPGA, and develop software on that processor to communicate with and control I/O devices.
- **[Computer Science Experiment b](https://syllabus.adm.nagoya-u.ac.jp/data/2026/10_2026_X320001001359.html)** (コンピュータ科学実験b): group experiments on assembling computers and electronic circuits, controlling circuits from a computer, and building and operating a small multi-PC network, including packet observation and security basics.
- **[Software Development Methods and Practice](https://syllabus.adm.nagoya-u.ac.jp/data/2026/10_2026_X320001001019.html)** (ソフトウエア開発法及び演習): software development models (waterfall, prototyping, spiral, extreme) and the tools that support large-scale programming, such as IDEs, debuggers, build tools and version control, with hands-on exercises.
- **[Object-Oriented Programming and Practice 2](https://syllabus.adm.nagoya-u.ac.jp/data/2026/10_2026_X320001001028.html)** (オブジェクト指向言語及び演習2): fundamentals and techniques of object-oriented programming in C++, covering encapsulation, inheritance, polymorphism, dynamic binding, the Standard Template Library and exception handling, with a programming project.

<h2 id="hosei">Hosei University, Graduate School of Science and Engineering (2024 – Present, Part-time Lecturer)</h2>

- **[Generative AI with Large Language Models](https://syllabus.hosei.ac.jp/web/preview.php?no_id=2514978&nendo=2025&gakubueng=EV&t_mode=pc&radd=)** (大規模言語モデルを用いた生成型AI): graduate course offering a comprehensive understanding of generative AI centered on large language models, covering the core principles of LLMs, practical skills for implementing and fine-tuning them, and their application in interdisciplinary research.
