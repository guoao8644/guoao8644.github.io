<h2 id="hosei">Hosei University, Graduate School of Science and Engineering (2024 – Present)</h2>

**[Generative AI with Large Language Models](https://syllabus.hosei.ac.jp/web/preview.php?no_id=2514978&nendo=2025&gakubueng=EV&t_mode=pc&radd=)** (大規模言語モデルを用いた生成型AI) &nbsp;·&nbsp; Part-time Lecturer, sole instructor &nbsp;·&nbsp; graduate course, fall semester, 2 credits

A course I designed and teach independently, giving graduate students a comprehensive understanding of generative AI centered on large language models (LLMs): the underlying theory, how LLMs are developed and fine-tuned, and how they are applied in research areas such as spoken dialogue systems. The course runs in two parts. The first part builds the foundations, from neural networks and NLP basics through the Transformer architecture, pre-training, prompt-based and in-context learning, chain-of-thought and RLHF, to fine-tuning and benchmarking, with an implementation exercise on emotion analysis. The second part is hands-on: students build task-oriented and open-domain dialogue systems, implement a spoken dialogue system on the open-source Remdis platform, study efficient fine-tuning (LoRA, QLoRA, adapter and prompt tuning) and crowdsourcing for LLMs, and finish with a final project and presentation. By the end of the course, students can fine-tune LLMs, build and evaluate LLM-based applications, and apply LLMs to their own research.
