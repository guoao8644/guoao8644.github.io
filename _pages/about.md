My research is driven by a vision of a **human-machine symbiotic society**: a world in which robots and AI dialogue systems live alongside people as genuine partners, understanding each user as an individual and adapting to them as naturally as a human companion would.

Pursuing this vision, I work in three complementary directions, all centered on one question: *how can a machine truly understand a person, and act in a way that supports them?*

- **Hospitable mobile guide robots**: designing service robots that guide and assist people in real environments, such as aquariums and museums, with the warmth and adaptability of a human host. This direction is supported by the JSPS Grant-in-Aid for Early-Career Scientists project [“Research on Improving Hospitality of Mobile Dialogue Robots”](/projects/) (2025 to 2028, Principal Investigator).
- **Multimodal user state estimation**: estimating a user’s emotion, stress, behavior, and intent from speech, text, and physiological signals, giving the system the situational awareness it needs to respond appropriately.
- **Personality-adaptive dialogue systems**: enabling dialogue systems to recognize each user’s personality and adapt their speaking style, strategy, and decisions to the individual.

I am a faculty member of [Prof. Ryuichiro Higashinaka’s laboratory](https://www.ds.is.i.nagoya-u.ac.jp/) at the Graduate School of Informatics, [Nagoya University](https://www.nagoya-u.ac.jp/), where these research threads come together in real-world human-machine interaction. I received my Ph.D. in Computer and Information Science from Hosei University in 2021 and worked as a Postdoctoral Researcher at Nagoya University from 2021 to 2025 before being appointed Assistant Professor in 2026.

<h2 id="research-interests">Research Interests</h2>

- **Hospitable mobile guide robots**: service robots that guide and assist people in real environments with human-like hospitality
- **Multimodal user state estimation**: emotion, stress, attention, and intent from speech, text, and physiological signals
- **Personality-adaptive dialogue systems**: dialogue strategies and speaking styles adapted to each user’s personality
- **User persona modeling**: long-term modeling of individual traits, preferences, and background, as opposed to momentary states
- **Wearable biometric identification**: identifying individuals from ECG and other physiological signals, robust across emotional and stress states
- **Personalized large language models**: adapting LLMs to individual users’ traits and preferences
