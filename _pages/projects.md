<h2 id="hospitality-robots">Research on Improving Hospitality of Mobile Dialogue Robots</h2>

**Principal Investigator** &nbsp;·&nbsp; JSPS KAKENHI Grant-in-Aid for Early-Career Scientists, Grant No. [25K21312](https://kaken.nii.ac.jp/en/grant/KAKENHI-PROJECT-25K21312/) &nbsp;·&nbsp; April 2025 – March 2028

Robots are increasingly deployed as guides in public facilities, yet a mobile guide robot often fails to keep up with changing surroundings and with users' needs while it is moving, and the hospitality it offers degrades as a result. This project develops technologies that allow a mobile dialogue robot to adapt flexibly to both its environment and its users so that it can offer consistently high hospitality, with the aim of establishing the foundations for mobile guide robots that adapt to environment and user alike.

*Keywords*: guide robots, autonomous navigation, dialogue systems, hospitality, context awareness

*Related publications*: [Exploring Factors Influencing Hospitality in Mobile Robot Guidance: A Wizard-of-Oz Study with a Teleoperated Humanoid Robot](https://aclanthology.org/2025.sigdial-1.37/) (SIGDIAL 2025) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [A Comparative Study of Human-operated and AI-driven Guidance with a Teleoperated Mobile Robot](https://aclanthology.org/2025.ijcnlp-long.87/) (IJCNLP-AACL 2025) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [Heart Rate Variability-Based Evaluation of Perceived Hospitality During Interactions with a Mobile Guide Robot](https://doi.org/10.1109/icsec67360.2025.11298100) (ICSEC 2025)

<h2 id="cyber-i">Research and Development of a Cyber-I Open Service Platform</h2>

**Research Collaborator** (Principal Investigator: Prof. Jianhua Ma, Hosei University) &nbsp;·&nbsp; JSPS KAKENHI Grant-in-Aid for Scientific Research (C), Grant No. [26330350](https://kaken.nii.ac.jp/en/grant/KAKENHI-PROJECT-26330350/) &nbsp;·&nbsp; April 2014 – March 2018

Cyber-I (Cyber Individual) is a digital counterpart of a real individual that is expected to approximate a person's behavior, and even mind, ever more closely as personal data accumulate. The project researched and developed an open service platform that collects and processes rich personal big data from many sources and devices to create, administer and model Cyber-Is, including a cloud–fog database system with smartphones as gateways, strategies for controlling Cyber-I growth, and studies on the protection and use of personal information. My contributions centered on smartphone-based personal data collection from multiple wearable devices and on personality and affect modeling from personal big data.

*Related publications*: [Context-aware Scheduling in Personal Data Collection from Multiple Wearable Devices](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/stamp/stamp.jsp?tp=&arnumber=7847301) (IEEE Access 2017) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [Archetype-based Modeling of Persona for Comprehensive Personality Computing from Personal Big Data](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/18/3/684) (Sensors 2018) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [A Context-aware Scheduling Mechanism for Smartphone-based Personal Data Collection from Multiple Wearable Devices](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/7917149) (IEEE CPSCom 2016, Best Paper Award)
